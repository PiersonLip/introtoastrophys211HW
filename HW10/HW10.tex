\subsubsection{Breakup Speed}
Given \dots
\[a_c = \frac{v^2}{r}\]
\[g = \frac{GM}{R^2}\]
We find \dots
\[v_{crit} \rightarrow \frac{v^2}{R} = \frac{GM}{R^2}\]
\[\frac{v^2}{\cancel{R}} = \frac{GM}{R^{\cancel{2}}}\]

\[\boxed{v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{R}}}\]
\subsubsection{Breakup speed from alternative function}
Given \dots
\[v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{R}}, \qquad  R \approx .01R_\odot \left(\frac{M}{.7M_\odot}\right)^{-1/3}\]
We find \dots
\[v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{.01R_\odot \left(\frac{M}{.7M_\odot}\right)^{-1/3}}}\]
\[v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{.01R_\odot}} \sqrt{\left(\frac{M}{.7M_\odot}\right)^{1/3}}\]

\[v = \sqrt{\frac{G}{.01R_\odot}} \sqrt{.7M_\odot} \frac{\sqrt{M}}{\sqrt{.7M\odot}}\left(\frac{M}{.7M_\odot}\right)^{1/6}\]

\[\sqrt{\frac{G}{.01R_\odot}} \sqrt{.7M_\odot}/c \approx 0.0121891c\]

\[\boxed{v_{crit} \approx 0.0121891C \left(\frac{M}{.7M_\odot}\right)^{2/3} \approx .01c \left(\frac{M}{.7M_\odot}\right)^{2/3}}\]
\noindent \textbf{\large OR}
Given\dots
\[v_{crit \textbf{A}} = \sqrt{\frac{GM_{wd}}{r_{wd}}} \qquad v_{crit \textbf{B}}\approx .01c \left(\frac{M}{.7M_\odot}\right)^{2/3}\]
\[M_{wd} = .7M_\odot, \qquad r_{wd} = 7 \times 10^6 \text{m}\]
We find that \dots
\[\boxed{v_{crit \textbf{A}} \approx 3.643 \times 10^{6} \text{m/s}} \approx \boxed{v_{crit \textbf{B}} \approx 3.583 \times 10^{6} \text{m/s}}\]
Thus, both of the equations return approximately the same answer

\subsubsection{Is this feasible?}
No shot\dots This is because \(\boxed{v_{rot} > v_{crit}}\), meaning that a WD rotating that fast enough to produce the pulsations would break up, as its gravitational acceleration at that speed would be less than that of the centrifugal acceleration.
